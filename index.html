TIP

Complete Step 1 before opening Pearson+.

1

Attempt a Homework Assignment First

This step verifies your enrollment and links your Brightspace account to Pearson.

Open Brightspace and your Calculus course

Log in to Brightspace and select your Calculus course under My Courses.

Find your Calculus course under My Courses.

Open the current week

From the course homepage, click into the current week (for example, Week 1).

Open the current week from the course homepage.

Open a Pearson homework assignment

Choose a Pearson homework assignment, such as Section 2.1 Homework, and attempt at least one question.

Open a Pearson homework assignment and attempt a question.
Why this matters: According to the course guide, attempting a Pearson homework question is the step that verifies enrollment and links Brightspace to Pearson before the eTextbook unlocks.
2

Then Go to Pearson+

After you have opened a homework assignment, sign in to Pearson+ with the same Pearson account.

Open Pearson+

You can search for Pearson+ and open the official Sign in | Pearson+ result, or use the button below.

Pearson+→plus.pearson.com

Open plus.pearson.com ↗

Open the official Pearson+ sign-in result.

Sign in with your Pearson account

Enter the email address/username and password for your Pearson account.

Sign in with the same Pearson account you use for your Pearson coursework.

Find your eTextbook

Your eTextbook should appear automatically under My eTextbooks once you have attempted the homework assignment.

Look under My eTextbooks for your course book.
3

Optional: Download the Pearson+ App

You can also access the eTextbook from a phone or tablet.

Download Pearson+

Download the free Pearson+ app from the App Store or Google Play.

Sign in with the same account

Use the same Pearson account. Your eTextbook will be available in the app, where you can read or download it for offline access.

Bonus tip — Listen while you study

The guide notes that the Pearson+ app includes an audiobook-style listening feature for many titles. When your book is open, look for the audio/listen icon to have the text read aloud.

Still having trouble?

If you continue to have trouble accessing your course materials, the guide recommends reaching out to your instructor or Pearson Support.
